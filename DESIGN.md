# DESIGN.md — Monochrome Futuristic & Tactile Glassmorphic Multi-Portfolio Platform (Kelompok 2)

## 1. Product Overview & Identity
- **Product Name:** Kelompok 2 Multi-Portfolio Hub — Sleek Monochrome Spatial & Industrial Aesthetic
- **Institution:** Teknik Informatika, Fakultas Sains dan Teknologi, UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta
- **Target Audience:** Evaluators (Pak Rizal Broer Bahaweres), tech recruiters, high-end design agencies, and AI engineering teams.
- **Unified Architecture:** A central gateway connecting three specialized student portfolios under a unified high-end hardware/spatial design language:
  1. **Danuardi Saputro** — *Machine Learning & Applied AI* (Data topology, ML models, Streamlit)
  2. **Anugrah Linson Putra Aditama** — *UI/UX Design & Industrial Visuals* (Design systems, Figma)
  3. **Azwar Riansyah** — *Data Engineering & Systems* (CLI, SQL pipelines, architectures)

---

## 2. Visual Theme & Aesthetic Direction (Inspired by Reference Design)
- **Concept Archetype:** *"Monochrome Futuristic Industrial & Sleek Tactile Glassmorphism"* (Apple Pro Hardware / Spatial Liquid Metal & Matte Ceramic aesthetic).
- **Visual Composition:**
  - **Cinematic Dark Upper Canvas:** Fluid sculpted topological 3D wave mesh with a floating central pearl/titanium sphere, representing computational neural fields and mathematical precision.
  - **High-Contrast Ceramic Deck:** Seamless split transition into an ultra-clean, ceramic bone-white (`#F5F5F7` / `#FFFFFF`) structured dock for data specifications and portfolios.
  - **Tactile Surface Treatment:** Frosted smoked glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), paired with ultra-crisp hairline metallic borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` or `#E2E8F0`).
  - **Pill Geometry:** Ubiquitous rounded capsule buttons (`rounded-full`), micro-pill tags, and smooth rounded card containers (`border-radius: 20px` to `28px`).

---

## 3. Strict Monochrome Color Palette & Tokens

### The Grayscale Spectrum
- **Deep Obsidian / Void Canvas:** `#090A0F` to `#0E1015` (Deep space black)
- **Titanium & Graphite Surface:** `#161820` / `#1F222E` (Smoked glass card surfaces)
- **Ceramic Bone White Deck:** `#F5F5F7` (Lower specifications background)
- **Pure Ceramic White Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Light cards, popovers, docks)
- **Specular Border Tone (Dark):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` (Luminous metallic hairlines)
- **Specular Border Tone (Light):** `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)` / `#E4E4E7`
- **Typographic Scale:**
  - Dark Mode: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Metallic Silver (`#D4D4D8`), Muted Zinc (`#71717A`)
  - Light Mode: Deep Onyx (`#09090B`), Charcoal (`#27272A`), Muted Slate (`#71717A`)

---

## 4. Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` / `SF Pro Display` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700).
- **Editorial Sub-headers:** Delicate serif touch / refined tracking for captions.
- **Monospace Technical Metrics:** `JetBrains Mono` / `IBM Plex Mono` (for GPA `3.96`, metrics `0.81 ROC-AUC`, and technical specifications).
- **Navigation & Pill Labels:** Small-caps uppercase with wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.08em`).
